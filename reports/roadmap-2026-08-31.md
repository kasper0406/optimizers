## How agents work here, and what to challenge

`CLAUDE.md` ground rules are binding; this section is the operational layer
learned in programs #22–#23. It applies to every agent (subagent, workflow
stage, reviewer) touching this repo.

**Working discipline**

1. **Pre-register before measuring.** Criteria, thresholds, estimator spec
   (burn-in, bias corrections, truncation, pooling, seeds) and the exact
   producing script are registered before the first confirmatory sample
   exists. Thresholds are proposed by agents, frozen by the human, in a
   freeze table with the mechanical consequence of each number stated on its
   row. No Phase B launch while any row is empty.
2. **Author → adversarial review → fix, then independent re-verification.**
   Anything that will gate a decision gets at least one reviewer whose job is
   to refute it. Reviewers flag, they do not fix; fixers verify each finding
   is real before changing anything and record rejected findings with
   reasons. The orchestrator re-runs tests and byte-determinism checks itself
   before presenting.
3. **Descriptive outputs only.** Producers print registered quantities next
   to frozen bars and emit outcome-map rows as mechanical lookups labelled
   "adjudication is HUMAN". No script ever prints a verdict.
4. **Commit before launch.** The launch package (code, configs, prereg,
   anchors) is committed so every results JSON carries a clean SHA at or
   after it. `results/` is append-only. Deviations are logged pre-launch or
   disclosed as amendments — never silently corrected.
5. **Verify feasibility on disk before ranking or planning work.** Check that
   claimed inputs exist (sidecars, checkpoints, data bins, venvs, the GPU
   itself — `nvidia-smi` first; the box has ONE 5090 and other sessions'
   servers may hold it). A "$0 offline" step whose inputs are gone is a new
   run, and saying so changes rankings.
6. **Peeks are disclosed, verbatim, or the surface is demoted to
   exploratory.** If any aggregate of the confirmatory surface has ever been
   read (including published tables on the same seeds/config), that is a peek;
   structure as Phase A (exploratory, disclosed) / Phase B (confirmatory,
   unmeasured surface).

**The standing challenge list** — every reviewer attacks these, in order;
each item below caught a real defect in this repo:

- **Vacuousness (the bbp-A2 class):** can the criterion PASS on a pure null,
  or FAIL on its own registered control? Demand synthetic branch-production:
  every registered branch produced on synthetic data, each only by the stream
  that should produce it. (#23: P1 and P3 both initially fired on nulls; K1
  initially fired on a correct pipeline.)
- **Null calibration:** is the reference distribution the instrument's own,
  or an idealized surrogate whose tail is orders off? (#23: AR(1) tail was
  132× off the instrument's published tail; repaired to a same-probe band
  contrast that cancels channel-common inflation.)
- **Estimator biases as registered objects:** burn-in (anchoring transients —
  ≥5, sensitivity swept), −1/n autocorrelation bias, truncation/K-stability
  (a verdict that flips with a "sensitivity" is not a verdict), pooling
  choice (median vs mean changed a branch), censoring points, silent runtime
  downgrades of registered defaults (bootstrap reps), MC error on null
  medians (ratios need CIs on both numerator and denominator).
- **Outcome-map completeness:** exhaustive and mutually exclusive, or states
  fall through to the wrong closure. Enumerate the cross-product.
- **Estimability:** does the registered statistic have a usable denominator
  at the design size (tail rates ~1e-4 × 3456 probes ≈ 0.3 events)? Power:
  can the design distinguish its own branches at this n and seed noise?
- **Ordering/provenance:** mtimes, config-vs-prereg edit direction (editing
  the prereg to match configs is the forbidden direction — if it happened,
  disclose it as a defect with both costs), seeds vs the dev/eval boundary,
  git SHA and dirty-flag in every result.
- **Granularity and frame traps:** a statistic read in a momentum-selected or
  otherwise self-referential frame can manufacture its own signal (Phase A's
  top-tier DC excess); a block-level read can miss the mode-level object
  entirely. Always demand the control at the other granularity/frame.
- **Confound axes stated in advance:** step-matched vs compute-matched vs
  trajectory-position-matched — pick the primary, name the cost, refuse
  post-hoc switches.

**What only the human does:** author `criteria/` (agents: templates with
blank thresholds), freeze thresholds, adjudicate gates and branch readings,
extend registered scopes (e.g. the lr grid), authorize cloud spend, kill other
sessions' processes.
