# Ideation Round Report — Wave-2 Program Selection

Baseline context: Muon-geometry speedrun harness, Wave-1 anneal decomposition (76% averaging / feedback remainder 0.022 / cos(v,D) = −0.68), two measured stability frontiers (airbench lr* ∝ B^0.35 [0.30, 0.42]; nanogpt batch-invariant over 8×), and the zero-pass invariant search. Twelve vetted proposals reviewed; five recommended, six benched, one rejected. Two proposals were demoted primarily on 2026 prior art discovered in refereeing (Sigma-Muon, Explicit-Gauge), one on lab-internal feasibility findings (DESB).

## 1. Recommended programs (ranked)

### #1 — Gauge Ledger: radial/tangential audit of the WD=0 hidden schedule

**Program.** Test whether the anomalous anti-alignment cos(v, D) = −0.68 between the anneal displacement and the constant-LR tail drift is a *gauge artifact*: at WD=0 with (partial) scale invariance, the drift D may be dominated by function-space-null radial components, and Muon's fixed-spectral-norm updates make weight-norm growth a hidden effective-LR decay η_eff,m(t) = η/‖W_m(t)‖. Phase A is almost entirely offline on stored fp32 Wave-1 artifacts: (i) per-matrix radial/tangential decomposition of v and D, recomputing cos after projecting out radial; (ii) parameter-free test of the perpendicular-update norm-growth law and reconstruction of η_eff,m(t); (iii) pure gauge rescale of the constant-LR Polyak endpoint to annealed per-matrix norms with val eval. Phase B (pre-registered now, gated on A): gauge-fixed Muon replaying the measured η_eff(t) as an explicit open-loop schedule must reproduce the constant-LR tail; a constant-η_eff arm tests whether the hidden anneal is load-bearing.

**Why faster/binding.** Either outcome is decisive. Pass: the anneal's feedback remainder acquires a mechanistic identity (LR schedule and weight-norm trajectory are one control variable seen twice), quantitatively closing escape route (i) for spectral-LMO training at WD=0 — something no 2025-26 paper measures — and it gates two downstream intervention programs. Fail: the anti-alignment is genuine functional opposition, killing the hidden-LR-schedule route for ~3 analyst-days.

**Novelty.** Partially explored. The *mechanism* (norm growth = hidden LR decay) is canon (van Laarhoven 2017; Spherical Motion Dynamics; Kosson arXiv:2305.17212; NaP arXiv:2407.01800 is the make-it-explicit design class; Muon-specific norm growth in Kimi arXiv:2502.16982). What is unmeasured anywhere: the radial/tangential audit of an anneal displacement vs constant-LR drift (WSD/river-valley papers decompose in landscape coordinates, never gauge coordinates), the gauge-rescale endpoint-transport eval, and the open-loop replay-equivalence test with measured η_eff(t). Pre-registration must cite NaP, Kosson, and modular manifolds and claim only the audit + replay, not the mechanism.

**Phase-A milestone (pre-registerable, per the tightened feasibility spec).** Pre-register the invariance roster first: exactly scale-invariant = Q/K (qk-norm at train_gpt.py:1088); V/O/MLP classified non-invariant. PASS iff all of: (a) radial fraction of ‖D‖² ≥ 0.5 on the roster on every seed; (b) |cos(v_tan, D_tan)| < 0.2 on ≥ 3/4 seeds, with ‖D_tan‖/‖D‖ ≥ 0.3 and bootstrap CI over matrices excluding cos < −0.4; (c) parameter-free perpendicularity: median |2⟨W_t, ΔW_t⟩|/‖ΔW_t‖² ≤ 0.2 on ≥ 80% of roster matrices, and zero-fit c_m prediction reproduces ‖W(t)‖² within 5% RMS; (d) ±10% pure radial rescale of roster matrices changes final val < 0.0025. FAIL on (a), (b), or (d) closes the route. Phase B gate: open-loop η_eff(t) replay matches constant-LR final val within 0.0025 (n=4 paired); constant-η_eff arm differs ≥ 0.0025; interpretation bounded by program #16 (mechanism claim only, not anneal replacement). **Cost:** Phase A ~3 analyst-days + <2 GPU-h (norm-scalar-logging replays, not full iterates); Phase B ~10-13 GPU-h + 2 analyst-days.

**Biggest risk.** Only Q/K are exactly scale-invariant in this architecture; if the empirical function-nullness gate (d) fails on the roster — or momentum-inflated radial components (AdamP effect) break criterion (c) for reasons orthogonal to the hypothesis — the clean gauge story degrades into a partial accounting. The prereg's roster-first discipline is the defense; the residual-stream structure is the hazard.

### #2 — BBP Alignment Frontier: zero-free-parameter random-matrix law for msign(G_B)

**Program.** Model per-matrix minibatch gradients as G_B = G + B^(−1/2)E with E from the *measured* (anisotropic) noise covariance; compute via rectangular free-probability/BBP overlap formulas the alignment curve a(B) = ⟨msign(G_B), msign(G)⟩_F/‖msign(G)‖_F² with zero fitted parameters, plus the momentum-corrected e(B) = a(B_eff) with B_eff fixed by the EMA variance-reduction factor. Sub-threshold directions decohere completely and msign promotes them to full spectral weight; a(B) saturation vs non-saturation then predicts frontier batch-invariance vs power-law scaling. Measured entirely offline: gradient accumulation at 3 frozen checkpoints per harness (step-963 prefix fork; grad-only, so the HVP OOM is irrelevant), split-half estimation, sub-accumulation B-grid spanning ≥16×.

**Why faster/binding.** An average-case speed theory that binds by construction if the law fits: it predicts Muon's critical-batch/LR scaling at unswept configurations from one frozen-checkpoint measurement, gives graveyard #2 a predicted threshold location, and feeds co-design ("does this normalization change raise the critical batch?" becomes computable). It is also the designated *noise-side* competitor to Central Flow's curvature-side account — their disagreement on the nanogpt invariance is itself diagnostic.

**Novelty.** Partially explored; the mechanism is published (OpenReview 4vzhqq5hpX states the sub-threshold-promotion story nearly verbatim; arXiv:2606.03899 has Wedin sin-Θ analyses of msign; Naganuma arXiv:2602.03001 is the norm-based practical competitor). Genuinely unclaimed: the closed-form zero-parameter a(B) curve from measured heavy-tailed spectrum + measured noise covariance, the lr*(B) ∝ a(B) exponent prediction, and the saturation-adjudication of batch invariance. Intro must concede the mechanism and differentiate from Naganuma head-to-head.

**Phase-A milestone.** The only proposal whose milestone the adversarial review rated **decisive**, with four tightenings adopted: split-half construction with propagated error bands (band < 50% of measured dynamic range for countable matrices); vacuity guard (≥ 6/12 matrices must show a(B) dynamic range ≥ 0.2, extend grid down in B until satisfied or declare untestable); e(B) with pre-fixed B_eff adjudicates the frontier criterion, a(B) the RMT criterion; anisotropic covariance committed, iid-isotropic diagnostic-only. PASS iff (1) RMT curve within bootstrap CI for ≥ 8/12 matrices (gate), (2) implied airbench exponent in [0.30, 0.42], (3) saturation classification matches both frontier tables. FAIL branches pre-registered: "noise is not free" and "non-saturated a(B) with measured invariance" both kill cleanly and informatively. **Cost:** <20 GPU-h on the working 5090; 3-4 analyst-weeks (budget the upper end).

**Biggest risk.** Freeness of noise relative to signal: gradient noise is Fisher-like and shares singular structure with G, so the free-convolution prediction can fail for extensive-rank signal — a live outcome, not a tail risk. Mitigated by the numerical-subordination path with measured covariance and by pre-registering the failure as a reportable result.

### #3 — Muon Central Flow: an EoS noise-floor theory that must jointly predict both frontiers

**Program.** Extend Damian-Cohen central flows to the spectral-norm LMO with self-consistent oscillation second moment Σ(t). Structural claim: msign's unit singular values make oscillation amplitude saturate at O(η) independent of curvature (deriving graveyard #3 rather than fighting it); the frontier is instead set by an additive oscillation noise floor L_floor(η, B) — a spectral-integral functional, consistent with null #5 — plus a sampling-noise term. One derivation must then jointly predict, with zero per-setting fitting, airbench's B^0.35 exponent AND nanogpt's 8× batch-invariance, with the regime assignment (sampling-limited vs oscillation-limited) *predicted, not assumed*. Stationarity-side observable S(t) = η·tr(P_r H P_r)/r is demoted to secondary (noise-corrected generalized sharpness, acknowledging arXiv:2603.05002 as the deterministic baseline).

**Why faster/binding.** The only program on the slate that could turn both stability-frontier tables from measured into predicted objects, explaining *why* the scalar invariant search had to fail. Worst-case bounds are off ~30×; a validated time-averaged theory is the one class that can bind. The pre-registered no-fit two-frontier joint test is the moat — no published paper attempts it, and bound-based competitors (arXiv:2507.01598, 2603.15958) are included as baselines expected to mispredict.

**Novelty.** Partially explored and actively contested: Muon-EoS via generalized sharpness is an ICML 2026 oral (2603.05002, full-batch); stochastic EoS corrections exist for Euclidean SGD (2412.20553, 2604.21016); central flows cover GD/RMSProp only. The matrix-sign central flow with a noise-floor functional and the joint two-frontier prediction has no prior instantiation. Fast-follow risk within 6-12 months is high — this program should start its derivation now even though the frontier comparison waits on data fixes. Note the review's warning: 2603.05002's Theorem 5.2 suggests η·λ ≈ 65 may be a wrong-metric artifact; the derivation must confront amplitude saturation directly (it must come from momentum + msign nonlinearity if real).

**Phase-A milestone (staged, per the improved spec).** Stage 0: commit machine-readable spec to criteria/ before lab-data contact — EoS-occupancy definition, regime-assignment rule, handling of the two data holes (no airbench HVP at B=8000; zero nanogpt HVP — new probes on prefix-fork checkpoints, dev seeds), held-out checkpoint list. Stage 1 kill-switch: derived flow matches direct msign simulation within 10% trajectory error on a 3×3 (η, noise) grid including η·λ > 20; stop on failure before touching lab data. Stage 2: hash-committed numeric prediction file (airbench lr* at every batch; nanogpt frontier ratio; excess-loss at existing rungs) before unblinding. PASS iff predicted airbench exponent ∈ [0.30, 0.42] AND nanogpt frontier variation < 1.25× AND excess loss within 2× at ≥ 70% of rungs, with regime assignment correct on both testbeds; S(t) secondary and unable to rescue or offset. **Cost:** 3-4 analyst-weeks + 10-30 GPU-h (synthetics, HVP retrofits, nanogpt probes — "pure reanalysis" was overstated; the stored spectra are 16+16 probes, not densities, and moment-matched reconstruction or a cheap Lanczos refresh is in scope).

**Biggest risk.** Closing the self-consistent time-average E[msign(M̄ + oscillation)] — a fixed-point matrix equation over fluctuating singular subspaces, non-smooth at crossings. The Stage-1 kill-switch caps the downside at ~1-2 analyst-weeks; the deep-linear closure may have to be demoted to numerics-verified.

### #4 — BARYCENTER: is the Polyak-to-SF gap a wrong-geometry artifact?

**Program.** Wave-1's sharpest unexplained number: SF anchored averaging beats open-loop Polyak by 0.022 (t = −88). Competing hypothesis to "feedback-requiring": the Euclidean mean of iterates whose singular subspaces rotate under Muon is the Fréchet mean in the *wrong metric*, blurring the spectrum. Compute four frozen constructions on the identical constant-LR tail iterate set (strided/streaming, seeds 1710-1713, step-963 fork): B0 recency-weighted Euclidean mean matched to SF's anchoring profile (the confound control); B1 subspace-aligned Karcher mean (reference frames fixed a priori from stored endpoints); B2 polar-factor mean via msign-of-sum; B3 dual-coordinate mean (non-trivial definition frozen pre-eval — the referee's degeneracy warning stands). Forward-pass eval at matched tokens, paired over seeds.

**Why faster/binding.** If any non-Euclidean barycenter closes the gap, the anneal's last mile becomes an offline post-processing step on any constant-LR run, and it pins the first statement of *which* geometry Muon's stationary distribution is flat in. If all fail (the literature-favored outcome, per arXiv:2502.06761), the 0.022 remainder is pinned as temporal/feedback with a pre-registered null — exactly the scoping the anneal-theory arc needs, and the no-dead-zone verdict map (PASS-GEOMETRY / PASS-WINDOWING / PARTIAL / FAIL) means every outcome moves the program.

**Novelty.** Partially explored: GeoMerge (arXiv:2604.27155) supplies the B1/B2 machinery nearly verbatim but only for merging separately-trained models; the 2026 SF+Muon papers (AMUSE, SF-NorMuon) all average Euclidean-ly without questioning it. The discriminating question — geometric vs temporal attribution of the averaging ceiling via within-run matrix barycenters — is genuinely unasked. Compute the inter-snapshot frame-rotation angle as a covariate (arXiv:2606.23637's angular-decay result predicts small B1/B2 gains if the tail is already frame-aligned), so either outcome is mechanistically interpretable.

**Phase-A milestone.** Precondition: synthetic planted-rotating-subspace unit test for B1 (SVD sign/permutation handling verified). Port strided snapshotting (stride ≤ 10, frozen; one-seed stride-sensitivity check); recompute C_polyak from the same iterates. PASS-GEOMETRY iff some B_{i≥1} improves ≥ 0.011 over C_polyak AND exceeds B0 by ≥ 0.005; PASS-WINDOWING iff B0 alone improves ≥ 0.011; FAIL iff all four within ±0.005 of C_polyak. Secondary reportable: parity with SF xbar. **Cost:** ~3-6 GPU-h + ~1.5 analyst-weeks.

**Biggest risk.** Graveyard adjacency in mechanism space: #1's equivalent-destinations and #14's tail-repair null both say recomposed-from-edited-frames weights are null-prone, and B1/B2 are recompositions — the honest prior is FAIL. The program is ranked on the value of the pre-registered attribution either way, plus its near-zero cost on artifacts two other programs already touch.

### #5 — Angular Speed Limit: symmetry-intervention test that the frontier invariant is norm-referenced

**Program.** Hypothesis: the frontier-setting quantity is the per-matrix angular rate ω_l = η‖ΔW_l‖_F/‖W_l‖_F — norm-referenced, hence provably outside the 8-absolute-scalar span that returned zero passes. Reframed per the novelty referee: the **primary** pre-registered claim is the cross-B collapse test — time-averaged frontier ω constant (CV < 15%) across the airbench batch range while lr*(B) moves >1.7× — which no paper reports; the init-rescale c-sweep (BN-preceded conv weights scaled by c ∈ {1/4…4}, log-log slope of lr* vs c against separated predictions c^0/c^1/c^2) is retained as the layer-attribution/mechanism experiment, with the four-verdict scheme including the pre-committed equilibration-corrected gauge curve (naive c^1 attenuates when accumulated fixed-Frobenius updates swamp c‖W_0‖) and the binding-layer check before any "norm-blind FAIL" verdict.

**Why faster/binding.** The frontier invariant is the lab's largest standing open question (null #5), and this is the first *intervention-class* attack on it — function-preserving symmetry surgery with separated predictions, not correlation screening. A collapse pass hands the lab the invariant and arbitrates gauge-set vs noise-set mechanics for the B^0.35 exponent, dovetailing with Gauge Ledger (same gauge object, nanogpt-tail vs airbench-frontier faces) and adjudicating against BBP's noise-side account.

**Novelty.** Partially explored, and the honest framing matters: c^1 for Muon on scale-invariant layers is nearly forced a priori, and the actionable optimizer variant is published (Hyperball arXiv:2606.16899; AngularMuon arXiv:2606.23637) — Phase B must be positioned as an independent matched-tokens stress test, must beat the tuned 3.28888 (not merely widen stability, per the #9 precedent), and must include a global-pooled arm. The intervention design and the frontier ω-collapse measurement have no prior art.

**Phase-A milestone.** Fixed B; c ∈ {1/4, 1/2, 1, 2, 4} × 6 LR rungs × 4 dev seeds (≥1000); pre-launch gates: numerical function-preservation check at init, fp32 telemetry at c-extremes (known fp16 silent-inf trap); log ‖W_l,t‖ and ω_l everywhere; pre-registered fallback restricting the fit to c ≥ 1 if norm equilibration collapses c-separation pre-shoulder. Four verdicts as above; collapse criterion CV < 15% via fill-in runs at the existing lr*(B) grid (prior runs logged no norm-referenced scalar). **Cost:** 15-20 GPU-h airbench + 0.5-1 analyst-week; Phase B if triggered ~10 GPU-h nanogpt.

**Biggest risk.** WD=0 norm equilibration destroying the discriminating power of the c-sweep (updates swamp the init rescale before the frontier is set), leaving only the collapse test — which is why the equilibration-corrected prediction must be committed before the first run, not derived after.

## 2. Bench

**Averaging Ceiling (open-loop readout optimum + certified ceiling).** Viable and it slots perfectly into the anneal-theory paper arc, but it should run *after* BARYCENTER settles geometric-vs-temporal attribution (they interrogate the same 0.022) and after its asset misstatements are repaired: the #16 airbench snapshots were deleted (regeneration ~10 dev-seed runs required), the stored HVP data is probe-level not a spectral density, and full nanogpt save_iterates is ~390 GB/seed and must become online kernel banks + sketches. The improved milestone (surrogate must reproduce the entire held-out #16 kernel-sweep curve, not two point predictions) is the right gate. Heavy prior-art overlap (Pedregosa-Scieur, NQM, 2502.06761, 2507.09846) means the contribution is a quantitative certificate, an increment rather than a question — real, but second-wave.

**NRSE (noise-renormalized spectral edge).** Kept off the top 5 to avoid funding two overlapping curvature-side frontier theories: Central Flow subsumes NRSE's target (both must explain the ladder and the two frontiers) with a stronger no-fit joint test, while NRSE carries a specific #5 hazard — its base quantity lr·D̄ was already screened to zero passes, and if the derived φ(ρ_B) is locally power-law with ρ_B collinear with screened feature ratios, the composite sits inside the failed span. Its near-zero-cost A0 (ρ_B collinearity pre-check on cached frontier cells) should be run *anyway* as a scoping probe; if Central Flow's Stage-1 kill-switch fires, NRSE's narrower per-singular-direction certificate becomes the fallback theory program, with its improved milestone (pinned model inputs, null-model comparison, cross-workload exponent test) intact.

**Explicit-Gauge Muon (spherical constraint + compensated schedule).** Correctly gated on Gauge Ledger and stays that way. Two findings from review force a rewrite before any launch: the compensator as proposed is inverted (gauge equivalence needs η′_t = η_t·‖W_0‖/‖W_t‖), and the intervention itself is now published twice with positive results (Hyperball 2606.16899; HyperP 2603.28743), with Phase B fully occupied by nGPT + Modular Manifolds. What survives is genuinely worth having: the exact per-matrix invariance census on trained endpoints and the anneal-decomposition question (does flat explicit effective LR + SF tail beat the record?) — run it as the intervention arm of Gauge Ledger's Phase B family, adopting Hyperball mechanics off the shelf, with parity pre-registered as an informative null (Wave-1 SF already gives parity at fixed gauge).

**CROSSING TEST (out-of-horizon anytime parity).** Cheap, zero graveyard collision, all artifacts verified on disk — but the binary question is no longer open: SF-NorMuon (2605.23061) affirms out-of-horizon SF+Muon parity at 1-8× Chinchilla and Hägele/Ajroldi document the failure branch, so a 4-seed result adjudicates nothing for the community. Reframed as "does the 24% feedback remainder grow with horizon" with the validity-gate design (rescaled anneal must itself beat 3.28888 before scoring), it is a fine ~10 GPU-h corroborating experiment for the paper arc — and the decay-placement warning (SF-spectral divergence without z-decay at long horizons) must be pinned in the prereg. Run opportunistically when the box is idle.

**DESB (depth-equalized spectral budgets).** The AdamW-only gap in the curse-of-depth literature is real and the improved milestone is exemplary (direct leverage measurement replacing the misspecified η/r_l model; three zero/low-GPU gates before any training). But feasibility inspection found the harness is not the pre-LN idealization — learned per-layer scalars already implement a >10× depth-varying rescaling trained with Adam at lr_mul=5 — so the modal outcome is "absorbed by the learned allocator." Run Step 1 (the ~1 GPU-h leverage measurement) as a cheap standalone datapoint; commit training runs only if Gate 1-3 all pass.

**Schatten Dial (per-module S_p geometry search).** Muon^p (2606.13867) already reports the global-exponent pretraining negative, and graveyard #1/#8 mechanics predict destination-insensitivity; the honest expected deliverable is the one-sided negative certificate, whose value is real but modest. The surviving novelty (per-module-class exponents, S_p-consistent LR recompilation deconfounding Muon^p's own confound, certificate-grade protocol) justifies keeping it warm, with the LR-confound guard and the boundary-optimum caveat from review. Consider stealing Muon^p's already-evidenced tail-curriculum cell as a single cheap branch first.

## 4. Portfolio logic

**Start immediately (GPU-free / single-card-light, this week):**
1. **Gauge Ledger Phase A** — ~3 analyst-days, <2 GPU-h, artifacts on disk. It is the cheapest program, resolves the strangest Wave-1 number, and *gates* Explicit-Gauge and interacts with Angular Speed Limit; nothing should queue ahead of it.
2. **Central Flow Stage 0-1** — pure derivation + synthetic simulation; the kill-switch verdict arrives before any lab-data or GPU commitment, and the fast-follow race risk makes the derivation clock start now. Run NRSE's free A0 collinearity pre-check alongside as scoping.
3. **BBP Phase A** — frozen-checkpoint gradient accumulation fits the working 5090 in background slots; theory assembly proceeds in parallel with Central Flow (they are designed adversaries: noise-side vs curvature-side accounts of the same two frontiers, and their disagreement is itself a pre-registerable diagnostic).

**Queue on the single card, then post-reboot:** BARYCENTER's 4 forked tail runs (~3-6 GPU-h) interleave easily; Angular Speed Limit's ~120 airbench minutes-scale runs are the main single-card consumer and can start once BBP's probes finish. Phase Bs (Gauge Ledger replay, any BARYCENTER confirmation, ASL's nanogpt kill-test) wait for the second 5090.

**Paper-scale arcs.** The portfolio deliberately forms two coherent papers on top of existing results. **Arc 1 — "What the anneal is" (Wave-1 + Gauge Ledger + BARYCENTER + benched Averaging Ceiling/CROSSING):** Wave-1's 76/24 decomposition, the gauge identity (or refutation) of the feedback remainder, the geometric-vs-temporal attribution of the 0.022, and — second wave — the certified open-loop ceiling and horizon-transfer test. Every arm reuses the same fp32 artifacts and step-963 fork; marginal cost is dominated by analyst time. **Arc 2 — "What sets the frontier" (measured frontier tables + Central Flow vs BBP vs benched NRSE, with ASL as the intervention probe):** two mutually exclusive mechanistic accounts plus a symmetry intervention, all pre-registered against the same held-fixed tables — whichever survives becomes the lab's binding speed theory; if both fail, the joint negative (frontier invariant outside curvature-side *and* noise-side functionals) is itself the strongest constraint yet published on route (a)/(c)/(d).

Recommended sequencing discipline: no program's Phase B launches before its Phase A gate is human-reviewed, and Gauge Ledger / Explicit-Gauge must share a single kill-evidence ledger so route (i) is not double-counted.
